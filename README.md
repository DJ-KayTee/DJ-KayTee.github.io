# DJ Kay Tee

Mobile DJ • MC • Karaoke • Sound & Equipment Rentals


Hi, I’m Kyle Tran, also known as DJ Kay Tee. I am a mobile DJ based in Chandler, Arizona, serving the East Valley and greater Phoenix area.
I provide music, sound, microphones, DJ and MC services, lighting, karaoke, and equipment rental services for a variety of events. I work with clients to understand their music preferences, event schedule, audience, and equipment needs so each event can have the right atmosphere.


My goal is to provide reliable communication, flexible music selection, professional equipment, and friendly service from planning through setup and teardown.
